TechNova Online Store Policy.

Returns. Customers can return a product within 30 days of receiving the delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. The customer pays the return shipping cost, unless the product is damaged.

Delivery. Orders placed before 2:00 PM are shipped on the same business day. Standard courier delivery takes 1 to 3 business days and costs $5. Delivery is free for orders over $70.

Warranty. All electronic devices have a 24-month warranty. To report a defect, fill in the complaint form and attach the proof of purchase. Complaints are processed within 14 days.

Payments. We accept credit cards, PayPal and bank transfers. For bank transfers, the order is processed after the payment is received.
